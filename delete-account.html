Delete Your RefClock Account & Data

App: RefClock · Developer: Raymond Liu · Last updated: May 7, 2026
You can request permanent deletion of your RefClock account and all associated data at any time using the form below.

How to request deletion

  1. Fill out the form on this page with the email address tied to your RefClock account.
  2. Press Submit Request. Your device's email app will open with a pre-filled message addressed to the developer.
  3. Send the email. (If your device cannot open a mail client, copy the message and email it to rayrayliu2007@gmail.com manually.)
  4. You will receive a confirmation reply within 7 days, and your account and data will be deleted within 30 days of that confirmation.

What gets deleted

The following data is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered:

  • Your account record (email, password hash, display name)
  • All saved game records (teams, scores, goals, cards, notes, timestamps)
  • Your authentication sessions

What is kept (and for how long)

DataRetentionReason
Email metadata of the deletion request itself Up to 12 months Audit trail in case of disputes or duplicate requests. Stored only in the developer's email inbox.
Anonymized, aggregated database backups Up to 30 days Supabase automated backups roll over on a 30-day window. After that window your data is fully purged from backups as well.
Local game state on your device Until you clear it Stored in your browser's localStorage on your own device — not on our servers. Clear your browser data or uninstall the app to remove it.

Request form

Submitting this form composes an email to the developer from your device. No data is sent to any server from this page itself.

If you have questions, contact rayrayliu2007@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
