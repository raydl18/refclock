Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 15, 2026
RefClock does not collect, store, or share any personal information. All data stays on your device.

1. About This App

RefClock is a soccer referee timer and scorekeeper app. It allows users to track game time, goals, and cards during a match. No account or sign-in is required to use the app.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. RefClock does not ask for, access, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or any contact details
  • Your location
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Camera, microphone, or contacts
  • Any usage analytics or crash reports

3. Data Storage

All game data (scores, cards, timer settings) is held temporarily in your device's memory for the duration of a match only. No data is saved to external servers or persistent local storage. When you close or reset the app, all data is cleared.

4. Third-Party Services

RefClock does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics platforms. No data is shared with any third party.

5. Children's Privacy

RefClock is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal data whatsoever, the app is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.

6. Permissions

RefClock requests only the following permission:

  • Wake Lock — keeps the screen on while the game timer is running, so the display does not turn off mid-match. No data is collected through this permission.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated date. Changes take effect immediately upon posting.
